Auto-Resume for Claude Last updated: 14/11/2026

Auto-Resume for Claude ("the extension") is an unofficial tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic. It helps you continue a conversation on claude.ai after a usage limit resets.

What the extension does with data

  • It runs only on https://claude.ai/*. It looks at the page in your browser to find Claude's usage-limit notice and the reset time shown in it.
  • It listens for you pressing Enter or Send in the message box, so it can cancel a scheduled resume when you continue yourself.
  • After the reset time, it types your continuation prompt into the message box and presses Send.

What is stored

Locally, in chrome.storage.local on your device:

  • Your settings: continuation prompt, safety delay, maximum retries, notification and theme choices.
  • The one pending resume: the reset time text, the conversation's URL path, its state, and timestamps.
  • A short activity log of state changes and reason codes, which you can clear in Settings.

Retention: This data is removed when the resume finishes or is cancelled (settings and the log remain until you change or clear them or uninstall the extension).

What is not collected

  • Conversation text is never stored or transmitted.
  • No passwords, cookies, tokens, account details, payment details, location, or browsing history are accessed.

Sharing

  • Nothing is sent off your device. The extension makes no network requests, uses no analytics, advertising, or tracking, and runs no remotely hosted code.
  • Data is never sold or transferred to third parties, never used for purposes unrelated to the extension's single purpose, and never used for credit or lending decisions.

Your control

You can edit or clear settings and the log in the extension's Settings page, cancel any scheduled resume from the popup, and remove all stored data by uninstalling the extension.
